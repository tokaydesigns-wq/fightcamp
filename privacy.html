FightCamp

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The short version: FightCamp doesn't collect your personal data.

There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking or analytics. Everything you log stays on your own phone.

What's kept on your phone

FightCamp saves what you enter in your browser's storage on your device, so it's there next time you open the app:

  • training sessions, rounds, effort and notes
  • meals, calories, protein and your daily targets
  • body weight entries
  • your fight camp (fight date, opponent, weight class and limit)
  • 4-week programs you start and the drills you tick off
  • settings such as kg or lb, round timer lengths and orthodox or southpaw

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to make a backup. We (the makers of FightCamp) can't see it.

Deleting it: clear this website's data in your browser settings, or remove the app from your home screen and clear its data. Once deleted it can't be recovered, unless you saved a backup file.

Backups

"Save a backup file" creates a file with your data on your phone. You decide where it goes and who you share it with. "Load a backup file" reads a file you pick, on your phone. Backup files are never uploaded by FightCamp.

Video check

When you pick or record a video in Video check, it is analysed on your phone by a body-tracking model that is built into the app (Google MediaPipe). The video is never uploaded anywhere, and FightCamp doesn't keep a copy: it's let go when you leave the screen or check another video.

What goes over the internet

FightCamp works offline once it has loaded. A few things do use the internet:

  • Loading the app. FightCamp is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives standard technical information when the app's files are downloaded, such as your IP address and browser type. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Looking up a fighter in Learn. When you search for a fighter, the words you typed are sent to Wikipedia to find them, and their summary and photo are loaded from Wikipedia. Wikipedia receives standard technical information such as your IP address. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Finding videos on YouTube. Only if you tap "Find videos on YouTube", which opens YouTube with a search for the technique. YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

Nothing you log (training, food, weight or your fight camp) is sent with any of these.

Children

FightCamp isn't aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone. Because your data stays on your device, there's nothing for us to collect.

Health

FightCamp gives general training and nutrition information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training plan or cutting weight, especially for a weigh-in.

Changes to this policy

If FightCamp starts handling data differently, for example if it adds purchases, accounts or syncing between devices, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
