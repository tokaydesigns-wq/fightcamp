Health and safety

  • Ringcraft gives general training, nutrition and weight information. It is not medical advice and doesn't know your health, fitness or injuries.
  • Talk to a doctor before starting a new training plan, changing your diet or cutting weight, especially for a weigh-in. Stop and get help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath.
  • Ringcraft never suggests water cuts or rapid weight loss. Don't cut weight without supervision.
  • Learn techniques with a qualified coach. The animations and drills show the general idea of a move and can't check that you are doing it safely.
  • Only spar or do partner drills with protective gear, a willing partner and supervision. Take care with techniques aimed at the legs and joints (such as the oblique kick) and never use full power on a training partner.
  • Video check gives automatic notes from body tracking. They can be wrong; treat them as a prompt to ask your coach, not a verdict.
